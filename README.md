Wickwarden is an original offline browser action game: three candle disciplines journey across four living realms to defend the last hearth. Choose a difficulty, weave lantern tripwires, carry embers home for fuel and camp supplies, draft thirteen kinds of kindlings, and defeat Mother Umbra before the light goes out.

All art, environments, effects and audio are generated by the game. No assets, dependencies or network are needed.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# Open http://127.0.0.1:8080 in desktop Chrome.
# Optional: node --test tests/*.test.mjs
```

## Keep the last light alive

- WASD or arrow keys: move. Mouse: aim. Hold left click (or J): fire.
- Space or Shift: dash, briefly invulnerable and burning enemies you pass through.
- E or right click: release a charged Sunflare, burning nearby foes, clearing their projectiles and slowing the gloam.
- F: plant a lantern seed outside the hearth ring. Up to three lanterns connect to the hearth with burning, slowing light lines; foes can break them, and their fuel lasts 34 seconds. Use them to intercept approaches or illuminate an ember run.
- Walk inside the hearth circle with at least three embers to bank them automatically: restore light, earn points and collect wax for camp. Remaining carried embers bank at the end of a night.
- After each night, make at most one optional camp purchase with Z/X/C or the mouse: Hearthwatch turrets, Fresh Tallow healing, or more lantern seeds. Prices rise as you buy the same supply; spend on survival now or save for a stronger defense later.
- Choose one free kindling with the mouse or keys 1–3 to leave camp. Thirteen upgrade types stack into different builds.
- Keep both your wick and the hearth alive for ten nights. A queen arrives on night five; Mother Umbra comes on night ten.
- Escape / P: pause. M: mute. Enter / R on the results screen: restart.

## Four realms, nine starting combinations

The Ashen Court teaches the ember loop. In the Glass Fen, shallow pools slow your feet and the gloam alike, while a dash cuts through. The Thorn Archive's roots telegraph blooms before striking; bait enemies into them. At the Aurora Crown, falling stars threaten you and the queen together. Each realm has its own palette, landmarks and rules, and the live tactical map shows enemies, your candle, hearth and lantern-line geometry.

Choose Wick's steady fire, Nettle's wide thorn fan, or Glint's swift chaining sparks. Firefly is forgiving, Warden is the intended challenge, and Eclipse strengthens foes and pays a larger final-score multiplier. On the title screen, left/right select a candle and up/down select difficulty, or click their buttons. A local garden journal remembers earned realm seals and victories by candle/difficulty; it is a trophy, not a grind or a paid advantage.

Each run uses a fixed encounter seed so learning the threats matters; your aim, shots, upgrade choices and movement change the fight. The hearth continually loses fuel; moths consume light and larger creatures latch onto it. Returning embers is not optional when the night gets hungry.

Everything under `game/` is the playable, self-contained game, served without a build step. The simulation, encounters, input, rendering, interface, audio and upgrades are separate modules. `main.ts` copies the game and real browser screenshots to `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (or `/output`) and calls the unchanged make.fun dossier library. The library is distributed with the competition kit; the rest of the source was written for this entry across the two recorded Hermes build sessions.

The maker screenshots show actual browser renders from a keyboard/mouse play-test, not illustrated mockups. `tests/play-policy.mjs` is an external testing policy, never imported by the game, which exercises the same ordinary controls. Run the Node tests with the command above; the bounty itself has no local scoring, and make.fun grades submitted entries on its servers.
